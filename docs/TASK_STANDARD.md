# Novel Worlds Task Standard

## 1. Core Principle

A Novel Worlds task should test whether a model can make sense of an unfamiliar situation and act effectively within it.

The challenge should come from building the right mental model—not from obscure facts, tedious calculations, tricky wording, or remembering a puzzle the model may have seen before.

When a model fails, a good task should teach us something meaningful about why it failed.

## 2. Task Types

Every task must primarily belong to one of the following four categories.

### Rule Discovery

Figure out how an unfamiliar system works based on examples, observations, or previous interactions.

### Messy Reality

Reason through information that is noisy, incomplete, contradictory, or poorly organized.

### Staying Clear-Headed Under Uncertainty

Recognize missing information, conflicting assumptions, impossible requests, and situations where guessing would be the wrong thing to do.

### Adaptive Action

Explore an environment, take action, observe what happens, recover from mistakes, and complete a goal with limited resources.

A task may involve several different abilities, but it must have one clearly defined primary category.
